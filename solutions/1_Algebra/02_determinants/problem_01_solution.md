### Exercise 1. Determinant of a 2x2 Matrix

## Question

Compute the determinants of the following matrices:

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 \\
5 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 7 \\
2 & 14 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Which matrix is invertible? Justify your answer.

## Solution

For the first matrix:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 \\
5 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix}
&= 3(4) - (-2)(5) \\
&= 12 + 10 \\
&= 22
\end{aligned}
$$

For the second matrix:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 7 \\
2 & 14 \\
\end{pmatrix}
&= 1(14) - 7(2) \\
&= 14 - 14 \\
&= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

The first matrix is invertible because its determinant is not zero ($22 \neq 0$). The second matrix is not invertible because its determinant is exactly zero.
