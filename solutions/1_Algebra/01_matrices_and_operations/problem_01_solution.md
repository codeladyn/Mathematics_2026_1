### Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries

**Result**

The size of matrix $A$ is $2 \times 3$ (2 rows, 3 columns).
The size of matrix $B$ is $3 \times 2$ (3 rows, 2 columns).

The requested entries are:
- $a_{12} = -1$
- $a_{23} = 3$
- $b_{21} = -2$
- $b_{32} = 1$

The second row of $A$ as a vector is:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 4 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The first column of $B$ as a vector is:

$$
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
-2 \\
4 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Explanation**

Matrix size is always written as "rows by columns". The little index numbers just tell you exactly where to look. For example, $a_{12}$ just means "go to row 1, column 2".
