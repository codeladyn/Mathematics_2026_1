### Exercise 7. Identity Matrix, Zero Matrix, and Powers

**Result**

$$
AI =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & 2 \\
\end{pmatrix}
= A
$$

$$
IA =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & 2 \\
\end{pmatrix}
= A
$$

$$
A+0 =
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & 2 \\
\end{pmatrix}
= A
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
A^2 &=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & 2 \\
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & 2 \\
\end{pmatrix} \\
&=
\begin{pmatrix}
4 & 4 \\
0 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
A^3 &= A^2 A \\
&=
\begin{pmatrix}
4 & 4 \\
0 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 1 \\
0 & 2 \\
\end{pmatrix} \\
&=
\begin{pmatrix}
8 & 12 \\
0 & 8 \\
\end{pmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Explanation**

The identity matrix $I$ is like the number 1 (multiplying by it changes nothing), and the zero matrix is like the number 0 (adding it changes nothing). When you keep multiplying matrix $A$ by itself, you can start to see a clear pattern forming in the numbers as they grow.
