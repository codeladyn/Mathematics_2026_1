### Exercise 8. Sphere

## Question

Reduce

$$
x^2+y^2+z^2-4x+2y-6z-2=0
$$

to canonical form. Find the center and radius. Give two points on the sphere and check them in the equation.

## Solution

Complete the square for $x$, $y$, and $z$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x^2 - 4x) + (y^2 + 2y) + (z^2 - 6z) &= 2 \\
(x - 2)^2 - 4 + (y + 1)^2 - 1 + (z - 3)^2 - 9 &= 2 \\
(x - 2)^2 + (y + 1)^2 + (z - 3)^2 &= 16
\end{aligned}
$$

- **Center:** $(2, -1, 3)$.
- **Radius:** $r = \sqrt{16} = 4$.

Two simple points on the sphere (moving $4$ units along the $x$-axis and $y$-axis from the center) are $P_1 = (6, -1, 3)$ and $P_2 = (2, 3, 3)$.

Check in the original equation:
- For $P_1 = (6, -1, 3)$: $6^2 + (-1)^2 + 3^2 - 4(6) + 2(-1) - 6(3) - 2 = 36 + 1 + 9 - 24 - 2 - 18 - 2 = 0$ (true).
- For $P_2 = (2, 3, 3)$: $2^2 + 3^2 + 3^2 - 4(2) + 2(3) - 6(3) - 2 = 4 + 9 + 9 - 8 + 6 - 18 - 2 = 0$ (true).
