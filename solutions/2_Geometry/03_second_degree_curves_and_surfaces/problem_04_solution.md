### Exercise 4. Parabola

## Question

For

$$
y^2=12x,
$$

identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, and opening direction. Find points on the parabola for $x=3$ and use them to make a sketch.

## Solution

From $y^2 = 12x$:
- **Vertex:** $(0, 0)$.
- **Axis of symmetry:** The $x$-axis ($y = 0$), because replacing $y$ with $-y$ leaves the equation unchanged.
- **Opening direction:** To the right (along the positive $x$-axis), because $y^2 \ge 0$ forces $x \ge 0$.

Substitute $x = 3$:

$$
y^2 = 12(3) = 36 \implies y = \pm 6
$$

This gives the two points $(3, 6)$ and $(3, -6)$.

**Sketch:** A smooth U-shaped curve starting at the vertex $(0,0)$ and opening to the right, passing symmetrically through $(3,6)$ above the $x$-axis and $(3,-6)$ below it.
