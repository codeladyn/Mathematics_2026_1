### Exercise 7. Classifying a Second-Degree Equation

## Question

Determine the type of object described by:
1. $4x^2+9y^2=36$,
2. $x^2-y^2=1$,
3. $x^2-4y=0$,
4. $x^2+y^2+1=0$.

In the last case, explain why there are no real points.

## Solution

1. **$4x^2+9y^2=36$:** Dividing by $36$ gives $\frac{x^2}{9} + \frac{y^2}{4} = 1$, which is an **ellipse**.
2. **$x^2-y^2=1$:** Difference of two squared terms equal to $1$, which is a **hyperbola**.
3. **$x^2-4y=0$:** Rewriting as $y = \frac{1}{4}x^2$ (one squared variable, one linear variable), which is a **parabola**.
4. **$x^2+y^2+1=0$:** Rewriting as $x^2 + y^2 = -1$, which is the **empty set** (no real points).

For any real numbers $x$ and $y$, we always have $x^2 \ge 0$ and $y^2 \ge 0$, so $x^2 + y^2 \ge 0$. Their sum can never equal $-1$, so no real point $(x, y)$ satisfies the equation.
