### Exercise 10. Paraboloids

## Question

Compare

$$
z=x^2+y^2
$$

and

$$
z=x^2-y^2.
$$

For each surface, describe the cross-sections $x=0$, $y=0$, and $z=c$, then make a qualitative sketch.

## Solution

**Surface 1: $z = x^2 + y^2$ (Elliptic paraboloid)**
- $x = 0$: $z = y^2$ (a parabola opening upward).
- $y = 0$: $z = x^2$ (a parabola opening upward).
- $z = c$: For $c > 0$, a circle $x^2 + y^2 = c$ of radius $\sqrt{c}$; for $c = 0$, the single point $(0,0,0)$; for $c < 0$, no real points.
- **Sketch:** A smooth bowl resting at $(0,0,0)$ and opening upward along the positive $z$-axis.

**Surface 2: $z = x^2 - y^2$ (Hyperbolic paraboloid)**
- $x = 0$: $z = -y^2$ (a parabola opening downward).
- $y = 0$: $z = x^2$ (a parabola opening upward).
- $z = c$: For $c > 0$, a hyperbola opening along the $x$-axis; for $c < 0$, a hyperbola opening along the $y$-axis; for $c = 0$, two intersecting lines $y = x$ and $y = -x$.
- **Sketch:** A saddle shape (like a Pringle chip) centered at $(0,0,0)$ that curves upward along the $x$-direction and downward along the $y$-direction.
