### Exercise 1. Recognizing Curves from Canonical Form

## Question

Recognize the curves:

$$
x^2+y^2=9,
$$

$$
\frac{x^2}{16}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1,
$$

$$
y^2=8x,
$$

$$
\frac{x^2}{9}-\frac{y^2}{4}=1.
$$

For each one, identify the most important features and make a sketch. Explain which parts of the equation let you recognize the type of curve.

## Solution

1. **$x^2+y^2=9$ — Circle**
   - **Features:** Center at $(0,0)$, radius $r = \sqrt{9} = 3$.
   - **Recognition:** Both $x^2$ and $y^2$ appear with the same positive coefficient ($1$) and are added together.
   - **Sketch:** A round circle centered at the origin passing through $(3,0)$, $(-3,0)$, $(0,3)$, and $(0,-3)$.

2. **$\frac{x^2}{16}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1$ — Ellipse**
   - **Features:** Center at $(0,0)$, horizontal semiaxis $a = 4$, vertical semiaxis $b = 3$.
   - **Recognition:** Both $x^2$ and $y^2$ are added with positive signs, but have different denominators ($16 \neq 9$).
   - **Sketch:** An oval centered at the origin, wider horizontally, passing through $(\pm 4, 0)$ and $(0, \pm 3)$.

3. **$y^2=8x$ — Parabola**
   - **Features:** Vertex at $(0,0)$, axis of symmetry along the $x$-axis, opens to the right ($x \ge 0$).
   - **Recognition:** Only one variable ($y$) is squared while the other ($x$) is linear.
   - **Sketch:** A U-shaped curve starting at $(0,0)$ and opening rightward, passing through $(2, 4)$ and $(2, -4)$.

4. **$\frac{x^2}{9}-\frac{y^2}{4}=1$ — Hyperbola**
   - **Features:** Center at $(0,0)$, vertices at $(-3,0)$ and $(3,0)$, asymptotes $y = \pm \frac{2}{3}x$.
   - **Recognition:** Both variables are squared, but they are separated by a minus sign.
   - **Sketch:** Two separate branches opening to the left from $(-3,0)$ and to the right from $(3,0)$, approaching the straight lines $y = \pm\frac{2}{3}x$.
